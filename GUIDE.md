# Emberfall — The Secrets Guide

**SPOILERS.** The game never tells you any of this. Coordinates are tile positions `(x, y)` on the named map; stand next to the spot and press **E**.
Everything below was read from the game's own rules, so the numbers are exact.

---

## 1. How the hidden systems work

* **Press E where nothing is shown and the game *searches* the spot you stand on.** Hidden quest steps are invisible "search spots". You must stand on the right tile, and some only work **at night**, **at dawn**, with an **item in your bag**, or once a **stat** is high enough. If it isn't ready, you get a vague hint (or nothing).
* **Quest points appear only when you are on that step.** A door, plate or stone that you can't see yet is simply not there for you.
* **Rumours and journal hints** are the only guidance: drink at the Tavern (Marta the Innkeeper), listen to NPCs, and read **Journal → Hidden deeds**.
* **Hidden classes replace your class.** You lose your old skills/path (Ascension is reset), so pick on purpose. **Mythic classes** replace it too, and **only ONE player on the whole server can ever hold each one** — whoever claims it first keeps it forever. The *Hall of Legends* (Character → Journal) shows who has what.
* Time: a full day/night cycle is **8 real minutes**. "Night" is the dark phase you can see on screen; **dawn is the short stretch of first light right at the end of the night** — if the screen is already bright, you missed it and must wait for the next cycle.

## 3. The Shadowfell (the second Lanternfall)

* **Entrance:** *The Sealed Gate* in the Drowned Archive `(28,38)`. It needs a **Shadow Key** in your bag (not consumed).
* **Shadow Key** — Blacksmith recipe (level 4): **1 Starmetal + 3 Iron Ingot + 2 Ink**. Only a Blacksmith can make it, so other classes must buy it from one.
* Inside: its own enemies, its own market, a **mana drain** (Archivists are immune; bring Lantern Salve / mana potions), Shadow Veins (Shadow Ore), Echo Crystals and **Shades** — mirror enemies that fight like you.
* **Echo Shards** (needed for mythics): Echo Crystal nodes (1–2, slow respawn), Shadow Wisps 25%, Shadow Husks 20%, **Shades 50%**, Void Stalkers 35%, **The Rift King always**.
* Shadow Ore → smelt **Shadowsteel Ingots** (Blacksmith lvl 6: 3 Shadow Ore + 2 Coal), used for Shadowsteel gear and the Titan Core.

## 4. The five Mythic classes (one per server, ever)

Requirement shared by all: **level 22** (Apostle: **level 20**), and the right base kind of class. If someone has already claimed it, the altar goes cold and you can't.

### Yggdrasil's Apostle — fighters (Warrior/Mage/Archer/Archivist/Desperado), level 20
Status **World-Tree's Mantle**: +1.4% max HP regen per second, reflects 15% damage, and **once every 5 minutes a killing blow instead heals half your health and roots everything nearby**. Skills: Thorn Lash, Rootbind, Seed Barrage, Bloom, World Tree's Wrath.
1. **Lanternfall well `(33,15)` — search it at NIGHT.** The water stops being water and a shaft opens (*"Under the well, the root remembers. Only when the lamps are lit."*).
2. Touch the **Well Shaft** door `(33,16)` → you arrive in the **Root Cellar**.
3. Read the **root inscription**: *"The root wakes as the sun walks — where it rises, where it stands high, where it sets, where it sleeps."* → **East, South, West, North.**
4. Touch the four **root-stones in the order E → S → W → N**. A wrong stone hurts you and resets the sequence.
5. Walk through the **Living Gate** (north end of the cellar) and kill the **Rootwarden** → it drops a **Heartwood Seed**.
6. Take the seed to **Gloomwood `(8,12)`** and search the spot **at DAWN (first light)**. (At any other time: *"The ground is willing. The light is not."*) The seed is consumed and a sapling grows.
7. Touch **The Sapling `(9,13)`** → you climb into **Yggdrasil's Crown**.
8. Kill the **Withered Guardian**, then touch the **Heartwood Altar**. You are the Apostle.

### Void Sovereign — fighters, level 22
Status **Voidborn**: 16% of hits phase through you; every kill gives a Void stack (+3% damage, max 15, fades after 20 s without a kill). Skills: Void Rend, Rift Step, Event Horizon, Null Barrage, Oblivion.
1. Get **5 Echo Shards** (§3).
2. In the **Shadowfell depths**, search the **northern obelisk `(24,6)`** with the shards in your bag. They are consumed, and *A Wound In The Air* opens at `(24,8)`.
3. Touch it → the **Void Rift**. Kill **The Rift King**.
4. Sit on the **throne**.

### Titan Forgemaster — Blacksmith (or Soulsmith), level 22
Status **Heart of the Forge**: everything you forge is +15% quality with a Resonance affix; enemies within 2 tiles burn for 4 dmg/s. Skills: Hot Tongs, Titan Slam, Molten Chain, Forge Nova.
1. Forge **15 masterworks** (items at **85%+ quality**). Quality comes from the forging minigame — heat, hammer and quench all count; level and a good Ascension path help.
2. Have **3 Starmetal Shards**, touch the **Cold Forge `(58,6)`**. They are consumed and you learn the **Titan Core** recipe.
3. Forge the **Titan Core** (Blacksmith lvl 22): **3 Starmetal + 5 Shadowsteel Ingot + 5 Echo Shard** (hard: difficulty 0.62).
4. Put the core on the Cold Forge. In total you need **≈7 Starmetal** (+1 for the Shadow Key, +1 if you also want Soulsmith).

### Philosopher's Heir — Alchemist (or Transmuter), level 22
Status **Gold From Lead**: brews never burst, +2 bottles per batch, +50% healing, 20% of your hits transmute a little gold. Skills: Acid Splash, Elixir Storm, Aurum Flask, Panacea.
1. Brew **15 perfect batches** (5-bottle batches — stir in rhythm, then be brave with catalysts).
2. Search the **paving stone behind the cauldron — the alchemist hut altar `(41,9)`** in Lanternfall. You learn the **Philosopher's Stone** recipe.
3. Cook it (Alchemist lvl 22, risk 20%): **3 Shadow Ichor + 10 Ghostbloom + 5 Ink**. (Ichor: Shadow Husks 4%, The Unwritten always.)
4. Search the altar again with the stone in your bag.

### Gilded Sovereign — Merchant (or Broker), level 22
Status **Midas' Ledger**: no listing fees, you never lose a haggle round, **+1% damage per 1000 gilt you carry (max +40%)**. Skills: Fistful, Gilded Cage, Coin Storm, Golden Parachute.
1. Make **5,000g market profit** and win **15 haggles**.
2. Search the **cobbles over the vault `(46,7)`** (Lanternfall, just outside the east wall of the Alchemist's hut). A vault door opens and demands **20,000 gilt tribute**.
3. Search it again and pay the 20,000g. Carrying a lot of gold afterwards makes you stronger.

## 5. The two hidden skill quests (open to every class)

Each rewards a powerful **class-specific hidden skill** (e.g. Warrior: Phantom Edge; Mage: Singularity; Archer: Spirit Volley; Blacksmith: Starforged Slam; Alchemist: Philosopher's Flask; Merchant: Market Crash), a title and gold. Nobody tells you they exist.

### The Weeping Wall (level 8+) — Quarry
1. Search the **cracked wall at the far south-west of the Quarry `(7,34)`** ("Knock where it is dampest").
2. Touch **the cracked door `(8,34)`** → the **Sunken Vault**.
3. Read the inscription: *"Third wakes the vault. Then the first. The second seals it."*
4. Step on the three plates in the order **3 → 1 → 2** (a wrong plate resets).
5. Open the treasure: **600g, the title "Vault Delver" and your class's first hidden skill.**

### The Ringer's Bell (level 10+) — Gloomwood and the Archive
1. **At night**, search the **bell-carved grave in Gloomwood `(52,33)`**: *"Buried without his bell. It lies where words drown."*
2. Search **`(8,34)` in the Drowned Archive** under the sodden shelves → the **Silent Bell** appears at `(9,34)`.
3. Ring it **3 times, about 6 seconds apart**. **500g, the title "Ringer" and your class's second hidden skill.**

## 7. Classes, Ascension and skills

* **Level 8: Ascend.** Pick a path (e.g. Warrior → Mana Sword / Brute / Bulwark; Mage → Pyromancer / Warlock / Sage; Archer → Ranger / Sniper / Windrunner; Blacksmith → Weaponsmith / Armorsmith; Alchemist → Apothecary / Bombardier; Merchant → Auctioneer / Caravan Master). **Level 20: Mastery** — a second choice that adds an ultimate. Un-ascending costs **150 gilt × your level**.
* **Levels 35 / 55 / 80: Advancements.** Your path grows past level 20: a new rank name, a stat tier and a new skill each time (three per path, 45 in all). They are built from mechanics — orbiting blades, rune rings, rotating beams, meteor rain, piercing lanes, chain lightning, vortices, leaping quakes, blink strikes, summoned familiars, banners, totems, wind walls, delayed bombs, shield domes — so each plays differently. Character → Ascension lists them.
* **4 skill slots.** You can only equip skills that fit your class. Sources: level-ups, **Skill Tomes**, your path, your mastery, **hidden quests**, mythic classes. Swapping is only possible out of combat.
* **Fighting above your level is punished** (you deal much less, take more, and enemies sprint to catch you). Fight at or below your level.

## 8. The economy rules nobody mentions

* **Crafters cannot list their own creations on the Exchange or sell them to the NPC vendor.** Blacksmiths/Alchemists sell to **Merchants** (who post wholesale buy orders and resell at their own price) or fill **commissions**.
* Orders are held in escrow, refunded, and expire after 24 hours.
* Everything crafted is **signed** with the maker's name.
* A **Broker** sees tomorrow's demand; a **Gilded Sovereign** pays no fees. The Merchant's haggle wins feed both mythic and Broker routes.
* Bots (customers/shoppers) keep the market alive when few players are online.

## 10. Guilds, PvP and guild wars

* **Guild:** 300g charter, up to 30 members, +2% XP per guild level for everyone (max level 10), bank (anyone deposits, only the leader withdraws), guild chat, message of the day. Guild XP comes from members' kills, bounties, deposits (1 per 50g) and raids.
* **PvP (press P):** level 5+, opt-in. Flagged players within 10 levels can fight outside safe zones; not party-mates or guild-mates. A PvP death costs **no gilt**. You can't un-flag for 15 s after a fight. Killing the same player again within 2 minutes earns no credit.
* **Guild war:** the *leader* of a guild (2+ members) declares war on another guild (2+ members, **their leader online to accept**). It lasts **30 minutes or first to 25 kills**. Enemy members are targetable anywhere outside safe zones, flagged or not. **Winner takes 10% of the loser's guild bank.** Tie = nothing moves. A leader can surrender (the other side wins). A guild that disbands forfeits. **One-hour cooldown** before the same two guilds can fight again.
* **Leaderboards:** Combat power, Total money (purse + escrowed order gold), Most valuable item, the six main-class boards (hidden/mythic classes are never ranked), plus kills, PvP, takings, crafts, raids, bounties, war wins.

## 11. Crafting minigames — how to score well

* **Forging:** (1) **Heat** — the thermometer starts in the middle of the gold zone and the game does not begin until you press **Space**. Hold Space to push it up, release to let it sink, keep it centred for 6 s. (2) **Hammer** — strike with Space/click when the marker is in the highlighted zone. (3) **Quench** — choose a bath (**Water → Keen**, **Oil → Warded**, **Brine → Lively**) and dunk in the zone (oil is the most forgiving, water the narrowest). Quality > 85% counts as a *masterwork*.
* **Brewing:** pick up to two reagents, stir in rhythm (purity sets potency), then press your luck with catalysts. 5+ potency = a **perfect brew** (counts for the Transmuter and Philosopher's routes).
* **Smelting** raw ore into ingots counts toward the **Cold Forge's** discovery (6 ingots).

## 13. Changes worth knowing (latest version)

* **Chests no longer hold gear** — weapons and armour come from Blacksmiths (or raid/guild-raid/boss rewards). Chests give gold, materials and the occasional Skill Tome.
* **Enemies chase until you are ~32 tiles away** (no more leash to their spawn), and after **10 seconds** without taking damage they heal slowly (2% of max HP per second).
* **Requests are the only direct sale for crafters:** open Character → Trading. Everything else a Blacksmith/Alchemist makes can only be sold through Merchants (wholesale orders / a direct trade with a Merchant).
* **Keybinds:** press **K**. **Tutorial:** Help (H) → Replay the tutorial. **Public world:** one global room; your character is also saved in your own browser.
* **Bows (for Archers):** Short Bow (Blacksmith lvl 2: 2 Iron Ingot + 2 Leather), Longbow (lvl 5: 2 Steel Ingot + 3 Leather), Shadowsteel Bow (lvl 7: 2 Shadowsteel Ingot + 2 Leather + 1 Echo Shard), Sunsteel Bow (lvl 11: 3 Sunsteel Ingot + 2 Leather). New Archers start with a Short Bow; ask a Blacksmith for the rest in Character → Trading.

## 16. Hidden and mythic skills (all unique now)

Hidden and mythic classes no longer borrow anyone's skills: **Archivist** (Inkblade Flurry, Margin Note, Living Lexicon, Epilogue), **Desperado** (Loaded Dice, Bluff Step, Dead Man's Draw, All In), **Soulsmith** (Resonant Hammer, Forged Soul, Ember Chains, Coldstar Nova), **Broker** (Foresight Volley, Margin Call, Leveraged Buyout, Liquidation), **Transmuter** (Quicklime Flask, Lead to Gold, Catalytic Surge, Grand Transmutation). Mythic classes replaced their last borrowed skills too (Titan Grip, Quicksilver Burst, Gilded Barrage). Hidden classes also have their own stronger basic attacks.

## 17. Bounties and customer orders

Bounties are now **accepted** at the board and **never expire once accepted** (up to 6 at a time; abandon any). Un-accepted offers refresh daily. Tell a town customer **"I'll bring it"** and the order is kept on the board for 3 days even after they leave.

## 18. Cinematics

Ascending, mastery, every hidden class and each of the five mythic classes play their own full-screen pixel-art sequence (click or Esc to skip after a moment).

## 19. Desperado dice

Before every attack except **All In** the Desperado rolls dice (one per projectile; one for the basic attack). Each die's face multiplies that shot's base damage (face x 0.28-0.3 of the old base, so the average is about the same, a 6 is roughly twice and a 1 roughly a third). The dice hover above the Desperado so you can read the roll; a 6 glows gold. Loaded Dice are thrown slower and fanned wider so each one is easy to follow.

## 20. The Far Reaches, the Spire and the new raids

* **Health ×10.** Everything that heals or shields you scales with it; flat life steal does not, so it matters far less. Old potions now heal ten times as much; the new percentage potions (alchemist, level 30+) are best for late fights.
* **The Endless Spire** begins at the monument in Greenmeadow. Floor numbers are real levels of difficulty: do not go past floor 10 at level 10. The titles Spire-Walker (25), Spire-Warden (70) and Spire-Crowned (120) are earned simply by arriving.
* **Raids:** talk to the Citadel Gatekeeper (a far region, level 40+) or the Wyrmcrown Warden (level 80+) with your party close by.
  * Citadel wing 1: kill Krosh quickly — at half health he calls thralls. Wing 2: the Oracle twins heal and rage when the other dies; burst the second one down within seconds. Wing 3: kill the acolytes, the Choir takes only a tenth of the damage while any lives. Wing 4: the Emperor adds a court at 66%, falling stars at 40% (the red rings show where; step out), and everything at 18%.
  * Wyrmcrown follows the same rules with five wings; the Worldwyrm is the longest fight in the game.
  * Raids reset if everyone dies; a raid is also ended after 40 minutes. Only one group can be in each raid at a time.
* Eight new ores/metals (Voidglass, Drakesteel, Celestine, Eternium, Nullsteel, Solarite, Mythril, Worldsteel) are mined in the Far Reaches and forged into gear for every class.

## 21. The ring continent, boss mechanics, upgrades and the log

* **Route.** Aether Reach → the Crossing (Lv 34). West ring = Mirelands, east ring = Ember Sands, both climb to the Gate of the Crown. The Citadel gate is on the eastern spur next to the Crossing; the Wyrmcrown gate is at the Crown's front door. Moonspire Heights has a pass into the western ring (a big jump in difficulty).
* **Bosses.** Read the red/orange shapes. *Wheel*: cross a beam with a dodge roll or keep moving. *Sanctuary*: run into a lit circle. *Grid*: stand on a tile that is not filling. *Walls*: use the green corridor. *Orbs*: they aim at you when released — move after they leave the boss. *Blink*: step out of each cut. *Closing circle*: stay inside. A boss below 66% and 33% health learns new patterns and overlaps them.
* **Critical forging.** Score 86% or more on the upgrade minigame and a successful upgrade may jump +2 or +3 in one go. The higher the score, the better the odds; the smith's level sets the ceiling (level 40: +2; level 85 or Mastery: +3). The ticket shows your odds.
* **Upgrades.** Success needs luck and skill: the minigame can lift a +40 step from 14% to about 18%, a smith's level and perks add a little more, and "Quick forge" skips the game. Do the early steps yourself and ask a stronger smith for the deep ones. Keep spare materials: echo shards from +20, starmetal from +50.
* **Adventure log.** Mastering regions earns the titles Pathfinder (3), Cartographer (12) and World-Walker (30).
